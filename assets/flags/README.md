# Clock flag assets

These 16×12 PNG files are displayed at their native resolution on the BUSY
Bar's 72×16 front screen. They come from [FlagCDN](https://flagcdn.com/), whose
flag artwork is based on vector files from Wikimedia Commons.

- `au.png`: <https://flagcdn.com/16x12/au.png>
- `de.png`: <https://flagcdn.com/16x12/de.png>
- `us.png`: <https://flagcdn.com/16x12/us.png>

Keep replacement images at 16×12 pixels or smaller. Larger images may be
scaled or cropped by `busylib` before upload and leave less room for the clock.
